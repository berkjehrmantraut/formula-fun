Ten Laps

Swipe up to go faster and down to slow. When the road turns gold, swipe left or right to take the corner. You start in neutral. Wait for the five red lights to go out, then swipe up into first. Shift up before they go out and you stall for a second. Every corner has a white turn line across the road. The gold leading up to it is how early your swipe can be. It grows as you slow down and shrinks as you speed up. A red line means you are too fast to make it at all.

Before each race you pick soft, medium or hard tyres, with no pit stops. Every corner wears them, and worn tyres shrink the gold. Swipe just as you reach the turn line to hit the apex: it saves your tyres. The car also gets quicker as fuel burns off.

On iPhone, sound only plays with the silent switch off.

Arrow keys work on a keyboard. A turn swiped with no gold under you costs a gear. At Interlagos it rains: the gold starts a third shorter and braking takes longer, but the track dries through the race and tyres wear at half the rate.
